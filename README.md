# Birth Order and Academic Productivity in Pre-Modern Europe

### Evidence from Brothers in Universities and Learned Academies

**Master's Thesis | Academic Year 2025–2026**

**Author:** Silvia Busquets Xapellí

**Institutions:** UCLouvain, University of Namur and Universidade Católica Portuguesa

**Supervisors:** Prof. David de la Croix and Prof. Hugo Reis

## Project Overview

This master's thesis investigates the relationship between birth order and academic productivity in pre-modern Europe, focusing on brothers associated with universities and learned academies.

The research uses historical biographical data and econometric methods to examine differences in academic outcomes between siblings.

## Research Methodology

The empirical analysis was conducted in R and includes:

- Data preparation and descriptive statistics.
- Sibling comparisons and regression analysis.
- Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) estimations.
- Family fixed-effects specifications.
- Robustness checks across alternative model specifications.

The analysis focuses on statistical associations rather than making causal claims.
