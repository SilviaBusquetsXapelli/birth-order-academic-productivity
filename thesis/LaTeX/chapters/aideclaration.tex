\section*{Declaration on the Use of Artificial Intelligence}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Declaration on the Use of Artificial Intelligence}
During the preparation of this thesis, I made use of generative artificial intelligence tools as a technical aid. Specifically, these tools were used to assist with writing and debugging code in R (RStudio) and to help correct grammatical and syntactic errors in the text.

The use of these tools was strictly limited to a supporting role. The research question, theoretical framework, empirical strategy, construction of the dataset, analysis, interpretation of the results, and conclusions are entirely my own work. All outputs produced with the assistance of these tools were carefully reviewed, verified, and edited by me, and I take full responsibility for the content of this thesis.
